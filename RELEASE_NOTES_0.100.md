# ParagliderVR 0.100

Initial public release of the Skyrim VR physical paraglider.

## Highlights

- Craftable paraglider requiring 2 firewood and 5 leather.
- Equipable carrier with dynamic biped-slot selection starting at slot 42.
- Two-hand HIGGS deployment gesture above the player's head.
- Physical glider mesh with lightweight collision and fixed lateral grip areas.
- One-hand flight fallback and configurable stamina-based flight controls.
- Landing recovery, active hand-height limit, wind audio, and OAR animations.
